## Goal and story

Add a fifth standard contract that makes the Nexus Dynamics faction lock recoverable through existing mission/reputation progression.

Nexus Dynamics discreetly hires the operator to enter a data center associated with Null Syndicate, recover an encrypted access ledger, and identify the route used to transfer restricted records. The ledger indicates that the intrusion may have been a cover for a transfer to a third party. This is a future-facing clue only; no branching narrative or new faction is introduced.

## Progression rationale

After completing job-01 and job-02 without all optional objectives, the player has Reputation 2 and 250 XP (level 3). Both contracts are owned by Nexus Dynamics, so Nexus standing is 2 - 2 - 2 = -2 (Hostile), which correctly locks job-05. Quiet Exit (job-03) is available at level 3; completing it grants one Reputation while leaving Nexus-owned completion flags unchanged. Nexus standing becomes 3 - 4 = -1 (Wary), so job-05 becomes available. This preserves the accepted HT-010 derived-standing formula and gives the player a legitimate recovery route.

## Topology

Seven fictional nodes:

- `entry` — External Relay
- `gateway` — Hardened Gateway (scan)
- `auth-core` — Identity Control (authentication)
- `audit-hub` — Audit Archive (metadata analysis)
- `data-vault` — Encrypted Data Vault (decryption)
- `mirror-node` — Traffic Mirror (hidden-objective discovery)
- `exit-relay` — Secure Exit (extraction)

Undirected links: entry–gateway; gateway–auth-core; auth-core–audit-hub; auth-core–mirror-node; audit-hub–data-vault; mirror-node–data-vault; data-vault–exit-relay. Every node is reachable from entry. The hidden branch rejoins at data-vault.
